One parishioner · three organizations · one constituent record

Meet Rosa Whitfield

Rosa gives to the Annual Diocesan Appeal (Central Administrative Offices), gives monthly to the Catholic Charities Food Pantry Fund, has a Capital Campaign pledge, reads the Herald, and volunteers at Diocese youth ministry events. In the future CRM she is one constituent with one Unique Constituent Identifier (UCI). Each organization still sees only what it is permitted to see.

Start as Rosa on her supporter portal, then switch roles to see the same record through staff eyes. Open Under the hood on any reply to see the topic, the actions, what the platform withheld and why, and the audit entry. Every step is labeled with the response code used in Attachment A:

  • SStandard (includes anything an administrator configures without code)
  • L/MLicense or module
  • CCustom
  • TPThird party
  • FFuture or Beta

Switch role. In production each role is a separate sign-in with its own permissions. This switch is not a "log in as" feature. It lets you compare what the permission model shows each role for the same record.

    Governance and audit trail (RFP 4.8.5)

    Permission boundaries

    Agents run as the signed-in user. Sharing rules and field-level security decide what each role sees; no agent instruction is relied on to hide data.

    Data privacy

    Agents never ask for or repeat card data. The CRM holds Safe Environment clearance status only, updated nightly from the screening provider (NCSI); screening details stay with the provider. Client services and case data are not in the CRM.

    Auditability & prompt logging

    Each turn records the user, the prompt, the grounded response and the actions taken. In production this is Agentforce generative AI audit and feedback data in Data Cloud.

    Opt-out

    Consent records with an "AI-assisted outreach" purpose are checked by a flow before any draft is written.

    Oversight

    Agent session tracing and Testing Center results. Merges and sends always need a person.

    Human handoff

    "Talk to a person" is always available and routes to the right office's queue with the transcript. The transfer is simulated in this concept demo.
